Transport and ownership
-----------------------
LAN and USB-C console reach the rig's SSH access layer.
The client forwards only the loopback service for a long session.
The rig owns the target lock for the full lifetime of that session.
Client disconnect releases the rig helpers and the lock.
The DUT USB cable is separate from the rig USB-C console cable.
